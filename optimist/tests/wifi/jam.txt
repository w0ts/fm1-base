# optimist/run.sh's wifi run). The air holds another FM-1 of a group (jam_peer) and a Link peer on it. No access point, nothing
# joined: the FM-1 goes to its group's channel, takes a 169.254 address and hears the other; Link runs although SYNC > LINK is
