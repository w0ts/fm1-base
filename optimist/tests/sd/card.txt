# The trial's SD card mode with a card in (run.sh's sd run: the profile copy profiles/sd-dev-07efe611.config, the
# build with FM1B_APP_END=0xCE000; the card optimist/build/host/sd/card.img, made by optimist/sdcard.py: FAT32,
# Optimist's card zip with every factory preset, SAMPLES/kick.wav and loops/tone.wav), on fm1-base's simulated card
# (FM1B_SD=FILE). Optimist's card code unchanged on plat_sd_*: the card mounted at power-on, its samples used and
# streamed, a factory preset loaded from it, a project saved to it by name and loaded back, the master recorded to it.
#   FM1B_SD=optimist/build/host/sd/card.img optimist/build/host/optimist_host optimist/tests/sd/card.txt OUT
wait 2000
